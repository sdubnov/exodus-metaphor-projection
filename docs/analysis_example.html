These plots are generated by the cleaned reference implementation. They reproduce the method and its didactic contrast, not the exact submitted paper figures. GitHub's normal file viewer shows HTML source. For interactive online viewing, serve this document through GitHub Pages or open it locally.

How To Read The Plots

Each point is a text chunk or paragraph. The color indicates the source text. In private/local runs, hovering over a point can reveal the corresponding passage. In these hosted figures, passage text is hidden to avoid redistributing the local research corpus.

The figures below show selected Exodus-corpus texts projected through a learned two-dimensional contrastive space trained from manually curated liberty-related anchor pairs. These plots ask whether passages become comparable under a shared metaphorical vocabulary of bondage, deliverance, law, covenant, trial, and self-rule.

Interpretive Summary

These plots illustrate the central methodological claim of the study: manually curated metaphor anchors can define an interpretive space for comparing passages across source texts. The anchor-guided projection reorganizes passages according to a curated metaphorical vocabulary of bondage, deliverance, law, covenant, trial, and self-rule.

These visualizations are exploratory and interpretive rather than conclusive. They identify candidate affinities for close reading and motivate future robustness studies over anchor choice, corpus composition, random seed, and projection stability.
